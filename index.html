What happened on Santo Oloroso

Six months after Captain Flint was found dead in his cabin, throat cut and a black crow's feather left behind, everyone who wanted something from his death washed up on the same small volcanic island. Here's how the night went.

The killer's tools, stolen in the first minutes

Almost before the storm had gathered, the Reverend Ian Plumpton (Matt) picked the pocket of the ship's doctor, Sawbones McGraw (Danny), and came away with his poison. Why would a ship's doctor be carrying poison? Suspicion found Sawbones far earlier than he'd have liked. (The Reverend, of course, was no reverend: he was Light-fingered Les, a thief in a borrowed collar.)

Bill Pistol remembers

For years the Diaho had looked after Bill Pistol (Barak), the pirate who helped Flint bury his treasure and lost his mind when Flint shot the other diggers. Sawbones sat with him and talked him gently back to his senses, and with his sanity came his memory: Bill knew the short way to the treasure, and the race for Flint's hoard was on.

Flint's treasure, dug up and scattered

Two sets of directions led to the hoard: Hayapitl's long way round and Bill's newly remembered shortcut. Saucy Nancy (Karen) got there first and dug up all six of Flint's treasures. A second searcher followed the other set of directions not long after.

Nancy didn't hold on to her haul for long. Two of the treasures were stolen from her:

  • The title deeds to Chole Hall, the family manor Flint stole, ended up with Wynn Harcourt (Courtney). The Governor's quiet secretary goes home a landowner.
  • The French map of the silver mine ended up with Jay Sommer (Lawrence), the stowaway.

Nancy kept the other four, until she lost them in the fighting at the end. With the hoard dug out of the mountain, Yocahu was placated, and nobody was sacrificed to the volcano.

Pardons: promised freely, given rarely

Sir Percival Stanforth (John D.), the only man on the island who could pardon a pirate, promised pardons to a great many people. In the end he signed just two.

The last stand

When the storm cleared and the voting began, Saucy Nancy was in the running for a ship. Then Shay Collins (Shubhanga) made her move. Loyal Shay was really Nelly Strychnine, a bounty hunter who had joined the Fury to collect the price on Nancy's head. She went for her captain, failed to bring her down, and fell, and with her Last Dying Breath she took Nancy with her. Her four remaining treasures were lost in the fight, and her claim on a ship died with her.

Who sailed away

Don Inigo de Velez y ContreraThe most votes on the island. The Spanish nobleman took the first ship, and only his valet knew the man at the helm was El Cuervo himself.
Hayapitl, chief of the DiahoWith Nancy gone, the second ship went to an unlikely captain on three votes: the chief who had wanted the pirates off his island all along.

Black Jack McCracken (David), Flint's old right-hand man, never stopped fighting. He went into six battles over the night and lost five of them, and the last one put him out of the game for good. He'd mustered a single vote anyway. Captain Overy (Alvaro) won no ship either, so there were no arrests: the pirates left behind are stranded, but free.

The solution: who killed Captain Flint

Sawbones McGraw did. As Flint's trusted doctor he mixed the captain's bedtime toddy, and the night Flint died he laced it with horned-viper venom. He had come to believe, from his studies of the sicknesses of the mind, that Flint was a dangerous madman who had to be stopped.

Later that same night, El Cuervo, who is Don Inigo, crept into the cabin and cut Flint's throat, leaving his crow's feather. But Flint was already dead: no struggle, no mark on him, and barely any blood. The whole Caribbean blamed El Cuervo for a murder he didn't commit.

Why not Flint's two lieutenants? Black Jack and Saucy Nancy were together that night, so each was the other's alibi, and they fell out so bitterly after his death that a joint plot is hard to believe. That left the one other person Flint trusted, with the opportunity, the motive and easy access to poisons: the ship's doctor. Motive, opportunity and means, as Sir Percival put it, and for that he offered his heartiest congratulations.
